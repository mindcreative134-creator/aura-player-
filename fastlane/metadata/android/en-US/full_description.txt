Aura Player is an ultra-fast, modern media player for Android with an all-in-one suite for Video, Music, Radio, and Online Streaming.

 - High-Performance Video Engine: Smooth 4K, HDR, and Ultra HD playback with Media3 hardware acceleration.
 - Ambient Cinema Lighting: Immersive soft letterbox backlighting for theater-style night viewing.
 - MX-Style Smart Gestures: Quick brightness swipe on left, volume boost up to 200% on right, and precision scrub seeking.
 - Complete Music & Audio Player: Background audio playback with lock screen media controls.
 - Synced LRC Lyrics: Real-time karaoke-style scrolling lyrics powered by LRCLib.
 - Online World Radio: Access thousands of live online radio stations powered by RadioBrowser.
 - Advanced Subtitles: Full ASS/SSA styled subtitles, external SRT/VTT file import, and multi-track audio switching.
 - Network Streaming & Downloader: Direct streaming via Stream Link (HTTP/HLS/DASH/RTSP) and built-in offline link downloader.
 - Clean Media Library: Fast media scanning with folder stacking, multi-item badges, and list/grid views.
 - Offline-First & Private: Zero telemetry, zero analytics, zero ads, and local-only processing.
